Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

This policy explains how CEASER AI ("we", "us") handles information when you use our website and app.

Information we collect

Account details such as your name and email address, the messages you send to the assistant, payment confirmations for purchases of points, and basic technical data such as browser type and approximate usage statistics.

How we use it

To provide and improve the service, keep your conversation history, process payments, prevent abuse and respond to your messages.

AI providers

Your prompts are sent to third-party AI providers to generate replies. Please do not share passwords, financial details or other sensitive information in chats.

Cookies and advertising

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in and remember preferences. We may show ads served by third-party vendors, including Google. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers needed to run the app (hosting, payments, email, AI providers) or when required by law.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and data by contacting us.

Children

The service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

Contact

Questions? Visit our contact page.
