AI and Your Privacy: What You Should Know

Be careful what you type into any AI tool, because conversations may be stored.

Many AI services store conversations to provide history and improve their systems. Read the privacy policy of any tool you use.

Avoid sharing passwords, bank details, ID numbers, medical records or confidential business information.

Use a strong, unique password for your account and enable extra security where it is offered.

Delete chats you no longer need if the service allows it.

Remember that AI answers can be wrong, so do not rely on them alone for legal, medical or financial decisions.
