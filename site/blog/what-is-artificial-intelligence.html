What Is Artificial Intelligence? A Plain-English Guide

Artificial intelligence is software that learns patterns from data to perform tasks that normally need human thinking.

Artificial intelligence (AI) is a field of computer science focused on building systems that can perform tasks such as understanding language, recognising images, making predictions and solving problems. Instead of following only fixed rules, modern AI learns patterns from large amounts of data.

Most of the AI you meet today is narrow AI: it does one kind of job well, such as translating text, recommending videos or answering questions. General AI, a system that can do any intellectual task a person can, does not exist yet.

The main approaches are machine learning, where a model improves from examples, and deep learning, which uses layered neural networks. Chat assistants are built on large language models trained on huge collections of text.

AI is a tool. It can speed up writing, coding, research and design, but it can also make mistakes, so important results should always be checked by a person.
