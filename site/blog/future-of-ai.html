The Future of AI: What to Expect

Expect more capable assistants, new jobs and ongoing debate about safety and rules.

AI assistants are becoming faster, more accurate and able to work with text, images, audio and code together.

Many jobs will change rather than disappear: routine tasks get automated while skills like judgement, creativity and communication matter more.

Governments and companies are developing rules about safety, transparency and fair use.

The best preparation is to learn how to use AI tools well and keep building skills that machines cannot easily copy.
