The Limits of AI: What It Still Gets Wrong

AI is powerful but can invent facts, miss context and reflect bias in its training data.

Hallucinations: models can produce confident but false statements, including fake quotes and sources.

Outdated knowledge: a model only knows what it was trained on unless it can search the web.

Bias: training data reflects human society, so outputs can reflect stereotypes.

Reasoning slips: long maths or logic problems can contain errors.

No real understanding or feelings: it processes patterns, not lived experience.

Best practice: use AI as an assistant, verify key facts and keep human judgement in charge.
