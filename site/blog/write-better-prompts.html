10 Tips for Writing Better AI Prompts

Clear, specific prompts with context and examples produce far better answers.

  1. Be specific. Instead of asking for a blog post, say who it is for, how long it should be and what tone to use.
  2. Give context. Explain your goal, your audience and any constraints. The more the model knows, the less it guesses.
  3. Assign a role. Asking the AI to act as an editor, tutor or senior developer shapes the style of the answer.
  4. Show an example of what you want. One good sample often beats a paragraph of instructions.
  5. Ask for a format: a table, a bullet list, steps or a short summary.
  6. Break big tasks into smaller steps and review each one.
  7. Ask the AI to explain its reasoning or list assumptions.
  8. Iterate. Reply with what to change rather than starting over.
  9. Tell it what to avoid, such as jargon or filler.
  10. Always review the result before using it.
