7 Ways Small Businesses Use AI Today

From customer replies to marketing copy, AI saves small teams hours every week.

Customer support: draft quick, friendly replies and build an FAQ from past questions.

Marketing: brainstorm product descriptions, social posts and email subject lines.

Content: outline blog posts and repurpose one article into several formats.

Admin: summarise long documents, meeting notes and contracts for easier reading.

Research: compare competitors and gather ideas quickly.

Data: explain spreadsheets and suggest simple formulas.

Planning: create checklists, schedules and project outlines.

Keep a human in the loop, and avoid sharing private customer data with tools you do not trust.
