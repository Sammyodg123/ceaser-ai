How AI Image Generation Works

Image models learn to turn noise into pictures that match a text description.

Most image generators use a technique called diffusion. The model is trained by adding noise to millions of images and learning how to reverse the process.

When you enter a prompt, the system starts from random noise and gradually removes it while being guided by the meaning of your words.

Good prompts describe the subject, style, lighting, colours and mood. Adding details such as camera angle or art style helps.

Be aware of copyright and consent questions, and do not use AI images to mislead people.
