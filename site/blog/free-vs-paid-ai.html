Free vs Paid AI Tools: Which Should You Choose?

Free tools suit casual use; paid plans offer higher limits, speed and extra features.

Free plans are great for trying AI, light writing help and simple questions, but usually have daily limits and slower responses.

Paid plans typically offer stronger models, longer conversations, faster replies and priority access.

Ask yourself how often you use AI and whether it saves you money or time. If it does, a paid plan may pay for itself.

Start free, track what limits you hit, and upgrade only when you need to.
